The framing in one minute

Test A: copy the simulator

Can a cheap model reproduce an expensive engineering model (CFD, activated-sludge, EPANET, PHREEQC) at acceptable accuracy? Scored against simulator output.

Test B: predict reality

Does any model, physics or learned, forecast what a real plant or water body actually did? Scored against held-out measurements.

A surrogate trained only on a simulator can't beat that simulator at predicting reality. Hybrids (physics plus a learned correction) bridge the two. The guiding principle: use the least expensive model that delivers the validated accuracy and reliability a decision needs.
